1. Coursebook Fidelity (Christina - Albania):

Authentic coursebook wording: Christina's narrative retains the authentic 2006 lexis ("natural disasters, such as earthquakes or tsunamis that happen along the South coast") matching Pupil's Book p. 2 and Lesson 1 Quiz #4 verbatim. This ensures exact correspondence between what pupils see in their printed coursebook and what they experience on screen.

2. Computer Lab Dialogue: Printing vs. Saving Source Discrepancy:

Source resolution: In the original printed Teacher's Book, the recording script wrote "I'm saving some photos" for Markos, but its own answer key corrected statement 2 with "he is printing some photos". In this digital companion, we resolve this discrepancy to "printing" to directly practice the target curriculum verb print alongside search, copy, paste.

3. Geography Clues & Extended Lexis:

Lexical scope: Several clue answers (FORESTS, EAST, WEST, NORTH, SOUTH, LANDFORMS, CAPITAL) are geographical concepts extending beyond the 35 core vocabulary items. This activity is clearly framed as "Geography Terms & Clues" to distinguish it from the 35 core syllabus items.
