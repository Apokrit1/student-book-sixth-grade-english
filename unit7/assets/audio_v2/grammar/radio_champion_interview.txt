Radio Host: Good evening sports fans and welcome to Top Stories on the Radio. Tonight in our studio we have a true champion, Greek Paralympian swimmer Konstantinos Fykas. Welcome Konstantinos!
Konstantinos: Good evening! Thank you very much for inviting me to your show.
Radio Host: How long have you been swimming competitively, Konstantinos?
Konstantinos: I have been swimming since I was eight years old. Swimming became my daily habit and my passion.
Radio Host: You won two gold medals and set world records at the Sydney Paralympics. What is your advice for young pupils?
Konstantinos: Never give up on your dreams. With hard work, patience, and determination, you can accomplish anything in life.
