Konstantinos Fykas is an inspiring Greek athlete who achieved remarkable success at the Paralympics. When he was young, his teammates gave him the nickname Dolphin because of his exceptional speed in water. Daily swimming became a disciplined habit that helped him overcome severe physical challenges. In the Sydney games, he was able to dominate both the fifty metre and hundred metre races. His hard training helped him earn international praise. Fans compared his courage to the ancient runners of antiquity, and his gold medal figure stands as an unforgettable accomplishment and historic achievement in Greek sports history.
