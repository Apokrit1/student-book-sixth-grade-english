Early in the morning, Alex and Maria prepare to board a giant hot-air balloon. The international contest was originally planned for yesterday, but clear skies today allow dozens of balloons to rise together among the misty hills. Floating silently high above, they witness the spectacular landscape stretching for miles. Alex uses his imagination to photograph the winding river below. They fly over a protected bird sanctuary where wild herons rest. A spectator on the grass holds a funny pet ferret on a lead. Near the landing destination, pupils deposit soda cans in a recycling bank while families enjoy roasted meat on a wooden skewer. More than one billion viewers around the world follow balloon racing festivals each year.
