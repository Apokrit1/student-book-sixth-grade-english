Elena and Giorgos love theatrical entertainment. Last December in London, they attended a famous long-running musical in the West End. The talented actors captivate the packed audience with live songs and energetic dancing. The composer wrote magical melodies that were later adapted into a popular movie. Back in Athens, their school drama society decided to revive a classic Greek comedy by Aristophanes. The student production received an enthusiastic review in the local cultural newspaper. After the show, pupils enjoyed a lively post-show discussion with the director about stage lighting and costume design.
