Sofia and Nikos love swimming at the Olympic Aquatic Centre. Today they watch athletes compete in a big national competition. Swimmers race in four main styles: backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly, and fast freestyle. The crowd cheers when the reigning champion manages to beat the national record by two seconds. Sofia watches the official time board to compare the results. In the final event, the mixed relay team swims with power, and two swimmers touch the pool wall simultaneously. The winning athlete proudly lifts her gold medal as the new record holder.
