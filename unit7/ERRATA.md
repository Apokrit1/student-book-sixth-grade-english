# Errata & Editorial Decisions — Unit 7: Share Your Experiences

## Document Overview
This document records editorial resolutions, print typos, and vocabulary enhancements for Unit 7 (*Share Your Experiences*) of the 6th Grade Primary English Coursebook companion.

---

### E1: Typo in Pupil's Book Appendix V ("feeestyle" -> "freestyle")
- **Source**: Pupil's Book Appendix V (p. 153), line 31.
- **Printed As**: `feeestyle` (phonetic / typographical error in Greek state edition print run).
- **Correct Form**: `freestyle` (noun / adjective referring to the standard front crawl swimming style).
- **Resolution**: Headword recorded as `freestyle`. Phonetic transcription `/ˈfriːstaɪl/`, definition *"A swimming race style where the swimmer can choose any stroke."*.

### E2: Hyphenation of "hot-air balloon"
- **Source**: Pupil's Book Appendix V, line 37.
- **Printed As**: `hot-air-balloon` (double hyphenated).
- **Standard Lexical Form**: `hot-air balloon` (compound noun: adjective modifier *hot-air* + noun *balloon*).
- **Resolution**: Normalised to `hot-air balloon` with cross-indexing.

### E3: Compound Adjective "long-running"
- **Source**: Pupil's Book Appendix V, line 39.
- **Printed As**: `long running`.
- **Standard Lexical Form**: `long-running` (hyphenated pre-nominal compound adjective).
- **Resolution**: Standardised to `long-running`. Definition: *"Continuing for a very long period of time without stopping."*.

### E4: Multi-Word Lexical Units ("packed audience", "relay team", "gold medal", "recycling bank")
- **Source**: Pupil's Book Appendix V.
- **Printed As**: Multi-word headwords.
- **Pedagogical Function**: Integral domain terminology for sports, performances, and environmental actions.
- **Resolution**: Preserved as complete semantic chunks with learner-friendly definitions under 18 words.

### E5: Konstantinos Fykas Historical and Pedagogical Context
- **Source**: Pupil's Book Unit 7 Lesson 2 (p. 88-89), Teacher's Book p. 94.
- **Context**: Konstantinos Fykas, prominent Greek Paralympic swimming champion (Sydney 2000 double gold medalist and world record holder, Athens 2004 silver medalist).
- **Pedagogical Framing**: Used as an inspiring authentic model for the Present Perfect Continuous (*"He has been swimming since he was eight years old"*, *"He has won several gold medals"*), overcoming physical challenges, and sportsmanship.
