A girl visits a department store to find a Father’s Day present. The class fashion-show activity asks pupils to walk like fashion models on the catwalk. Its table gives two examples: a cute, pink sweater and smart, dark blue, denim jeans. The official word list spells the sweater material woolen, while the Pupil’s Book table prints woollen. The table also lists the adjectives baggy, tight and loose, the elegant material silk, and items including a skirt, a sweater and a track suit. The material column includes cotton, and one table sentence says, “They match his T-shirt.” Before the listening, pupils look at receipts from different shops and choose one item as a present. The black shirt costs $30.95, and its colour does not suit the girl’s father, so it is too expensive. She chooses the green cotton T-shirt because it feels soft and matches his linen jacket.
