In a charming modern adaptation of an old fairy tale, the famous wolf sends a troubled letter to the school problem page. He writes about his misunderstood background: while villagers think he is frightening, he only loves playing the brass trumpet. Counselor Clara writes back with caring advice. She explains that if he apologises sincerely for past mistakes, people will forgive him and learn to trust him again. The wolf follows her guidance, holds an open rehearsal in the village square, and proves that mutual trust and music can turn a lonely wolf into a beloved friend.
