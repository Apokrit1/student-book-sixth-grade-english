Maya and Liam are passionate members of a popular school rock band called The Camden Sparks. Every Tuesday, they meet in the music room to rehearse and compose. Maya is the lead vocal singer; she loves to write lyrics that inspire her friends, with catchy verses and a memorable chorus. Liam plays the electric guitar with dazzling speed, while Ben hits the percussion drum kit to establish a tight rhythm and steady beat. Their music teacher helped them register for the city music festival and make a hall reservation at a famous downtown venue. The band creates a rich melody and harmony, and is thrilled to perform on the main auditorium stage for their big annual performance.
