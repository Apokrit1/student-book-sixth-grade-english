Eleni and Dimitris visit the Museum of Greek Folk Musical Instruments in downtown Athens with adult chaperones. The educational exhibition displays how folk music originated centuries ago and was passed down through each generation. A helpful instructor gives the pupils a colourful brochure and detailed handouts about each traditional instrument. Dimitris looks at an antique wooden oboe and a rare wind pipe, while Eleni examines a handmade string violin used at a traditional island wedding. The guide explains the length of each tube and how artists play instrumental melodies without electric amplification. The excited children look forward to attending a live concert in the garden tonight.
