George and Kelly receive a weekly pocket money allowance from their parents for helping with each household chore. In the past, Kelly would pester her mother for extra coins, simply to waste them on useless snacks and fancy toiletries. After studying how a smart consumer acts, the children decided to do intelligent research on financial habits. They learned that even a wealthy millionaire must track every bill and never owe money carelessly. Their parents provide three clear jars to organise their small income: one for essential needs, one for long-term savings, and one to donate. George managed to persuade Kelly to save approximately forty euros from every income source to buy school supplies for local children.
