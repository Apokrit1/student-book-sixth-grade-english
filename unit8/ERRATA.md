# Errata & Editorial Decisions — Unit 8: Blow Your Own Trumpet

## Document Overview
This document records editorial resolutions, print typos, layout run-ons, and pedagogical adaptations for Unit 8 (*Blow Your Own Trumpet*) of the 6th Grade Primary English Coursebook companion.

---

### E1: Typo in Pupil's Book Appendix V ("instumental" -> "instrumental")
- **Source**: Pupil's Book Appendix V (p. 161), line 34.
- **Printed As**: `instumental`.
- **Correct Form**: `instrumental` (adjective / noun describing music played only by instruments without vocals).
- **Resolution**: Corrected to `instrumental` (`/ˌɪnstrəˈmentl/`). Definition: *"Music played using musical instruments without any singing voices."*.

### E2: Typo in Pupil's Book Appendix V ("perfomance" -> "performance")
- **Source**: Pupil's Book Appendix V (p. 161), line 46.
- **Printed As**: `perfomance`.
- **Correct Form**: `performance` (noun: the act of performing a play, concert, or song on stage).
- **Resolution**: Corrected to `performance` (`/pəˈfɔːməns/`). Definition: *"The act of acting, dancing, or playing music in front of an audience."*.

### E3: InDesign Print Run-on ("attendsinger" -> "attend" & "singer")
- **Source**: Pupil's Book Appendix V (p. 161), column 4, line 146.
- **Printed As**: `attendsinger` (layout error where the verb `attend` and noun `singer` merged into a single word token).
- **Resolution**: Resolved into standard headwords `attend` (verb: to go to an event, concert, or music festival) and `singer` (noun: a person who sings songs). In the dataset, `attend` is maintained as the primary entry (`/əˈtend/`, *"To go to or be present at an event, festival, or meeting."*) with `singer` fully supported across Lesson 1 and Lesson 2 contexts.

### E4: Pedagogical Framing of Popular Cultural Music (ABBA's "Money, Money, Money")
- **Context**: Pupil's Book Unit 8 Lesson 2 (p. 100), "A rich man's world".
- **Legal & Educational Status**: Open-source educational derivative of public school coursebook under non-profit educational fair use.
- **Pedagogical Treatment**:
  - The song is studied strictly for grammatical and communicative analysis of **Conditionals Type 2** (*"If I had a little money, I would..."*).
  - Rather than synthesizing copyrighted full-length songs, the companion focuses on pre/post-listening tasks, comprehension questions on money management vs. consumer pressure ("pester power"), and guided student surveys on pocket money.
