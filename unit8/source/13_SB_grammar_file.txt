UNIT 8
Conditional Sentences

FORM

USE

IF-clause

Result clause

Type 1

Simple Present tense

will, can, must, + bare infinitive or
imperative

Type 2

Simple Past tense

would + bare infinitive

We use Type 1 Conditional Sentences to show that something is possible to happen in

the present or future.
		We use Type 2 Conditional Sentences to show that something is very unlikely to happen
in the present or future.
		
This kind of sentences often expresses a wish or advice.
