On Sunday afternoon, our town community council gathered to discuss how to protect our local bay and public health. An environmental scientist showed graphs proving that careless factories used to dump untreated chemical waste into the river that flows out into the blue ocean. She explained that heavy industrial plants must quit releasing harmful toxins into underground soil, because dangerous chemical substances can act as a deadly poison in fish and water animals. Even worse, long exposure to toxic waste and airborne chemicals can cause serious cancer in humans. Fortunately, our town has adopted green changes: businesses now use clean electric fuel for transport, and every neighbourhood has an electric recycling truck that collects sorted bottles and cans. Even when the sea is rough with autumn gales, local fishing crews report cleaner waters and healthy fish, showing that our determination to protect our environment makes a real difference.
