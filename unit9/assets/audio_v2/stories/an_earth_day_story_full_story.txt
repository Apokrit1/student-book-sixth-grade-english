On a sunny Saturday morning, our 6th-grade class arrived at the coastal beach to celebrate Earth Day. Before we arrived, a stormy winter tide had washed up heaps of plastic cups and broken fishing nets along the shore. We could see thick seaweed and tangled ropes cover the quiet sand where sea birds walked. Our science teacher, Mr Andreas, looked at the beach and told us our plan: we needed to get rid of every piece of dangerous rubbish before the evening tide came in. We put on protective work gloves and began to bend down across the warm dunes, filling big green sacks. Andreas smiled and gave a gentle nod when he saw Maria rescue a bright orange starfish that had been trapped beneath wet wood. Together, we worked for three energetic hours to save the beach and protect the natural environment so that local wildlife could thrive.
