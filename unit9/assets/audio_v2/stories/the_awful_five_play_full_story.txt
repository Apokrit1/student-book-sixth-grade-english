In our school auditorium, the English drama club rehearsed the famous environmental play, 'The Awful Five.' Kostas played Harry Wheezer, a coughing villain who loves smoke, while Maya and Liam dressed in dark cloaks as Carbon Monoxide and Sulphur Dioxide. On stage, Kostas began to brag: 'We are the nastiest gang on Earth! We come from every smoking chimney, old coal power plant, and busy industrial dry cleaner.' Maya stepped forward, acting proud: 'I am carbon monoxide! I am an invisible gas that steals your clean breath when traffic jams fill the avenue.' Then Liam shook his cardboard lightning bolts: 'And I am sulphur dioxide; when power stations burn dirty coal, I turn clouds into sour acid rain that can destroy green forests.' The audience began to stare at the comic villains with big eyes, laughing and learning how each chemical plant releases dangerous pollutant gases that create heavy air pollution across modern towns.
