# Errata & Editorial Decisions — Unit 9: Earth Day Everyday

## Document Overview
This document records editorial resolutions, print typos, layout run-ons, and pedagogical adaptations for Unit 9 (*Earth Day Everyday*) of the 6th Grade Primary English Coursebook companion.

---

### E1: InDesign Print Run-on ("dry cleanercause" -> "dry cleaner" & "cause")
- **Source**: Pupil's Book Appendix V (p. 162), column 1, line 21.
- **Printed As**: `dry cleanercause` (InDesign typographical layout error where the noun `dry cleaner` and verb/noun `cause` merged into a single word token).
- **Correct Form**: `dry cleaner` (noun: a shop where clothes are cleaned with chemical fluids rather than water) and `cause` (verb/noun: to make something happen / the reason something happens).
- **Resolution**: Both items are preserved and distinctly cataloged:
  - `dry cleaner` (`/ˌdraɪ ˈkliːnə/`, id 16): *"a shop where people clean clothes without using water"*.
  - `cause` (`/kɔːz/`, id 17): *"to make something happen, especially something bad"*.
  Both are naturally contextualized in Lesson 3's "The Awful Five" play and environmental causes discussion.

### E2: Scientific Accuracy and Pedagogical Sensitivity: Acid Rain & Air Pollutants
- **Context**: Pupil's Book Unit 9 Lesson 3 (p. 116), "The Awful Five" play script.
- **Pedagogical Treatment**:
  - The script personifies chemical pollutants (Carbon Monoxide, Sulphur Dioxide, Toxins, Harry Wheezer) for dramatic role-play in a 6th-grade classroom.
  - The companion balances playful theatrical dialogue with scientifically accurate environmental understanding (e.g. coal smoke causing acid rain, importance of renewable energy, reducing single-use plastic waste).
  - In student-facing stories, dialogues match the coursebook register verbatim (CEFR A2). Detailed chemical explanations and historical legislation (such as clean air acts) are reserved for `teacher_notes`.

### E3: Endangered Species Protection in Greece (Loggerhead Sea Turtles & Monk Seals)
- **Context**: Pupil's Book Unit 9 Lesson 2 (p. 112), "Save the Endangered Species".
- **Pedagogical Treatment**:
  - The book references loggerhead sea turtles (*Caretta caretta*) nesting on Greek beaches (e.g., Zakynthos, Peloponnese) and monk seals (*Monachus monachus*).
  - The companion connects pupils with authentic conservation practices: quiet beaches at night, protecting turtle nests, not leaving plastic rubbish, and volunteering at environmental rescue centers.
