Authentic Real-World Photography for Real Places

Whenever a unit explores real countries, rivers, mountains, or historical landmarks, we never use synthetic AI scenery. Students inspect real, freely licensed documentary photographs with full author attribution.

The Companion

A vocabulary-only layer attached to each unit. Flashcards, pronunciation, and lexical activities. The only place Greek lexical items are allowed and practiced — the bridge that maps new English words onto the Greek you already speak.

  • Vocabulary flashcards & lexical-pairing drills
