## Rights: material the app must NOT reproduce

The printed book was licensed for print. The app is a new publication and needs its own rights, so:

| Unit | Item in the book | Rule for the app |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Lyrics of The Beatles' "Yesterday" | Do not include the lyrics, a lyric gap-fill, or any TTS reading or singing of them. Build pre- and post-listening tasks (vocabulary, questions about the 1960s, "used to" sentences about the era) and a teacher note: "Play the song from a licensed source." |
| 8 | Lyrics of ABBA's "Money, Money, Money" | Same rule. Keep the conditional and money tasks; the teacher plays the song from a licensed source. |
| 3 | Shrek, Tinkerbell (and similar film characters) | Names in text are fine. Do not generate images, SVGs or icons that depict them. Use original creatures. |
| 10 | James Bond / 007, film posters, book covers | Names and factual text are fine. No depiction of the character, logos or posters. |
| all | Photos and illustrations in the book | Not reusable. Use generated original visuals only. |
| all | Pupil's Book, Workbook, Teacher's Book text | Coursebook texts are Ministry/ITYE material. Adapted text for classroom use is the project's premise; if you ever publish (e.g. Photodentro), confirm the licence of the source books first. |
