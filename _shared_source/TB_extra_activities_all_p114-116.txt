2. Read the following poem and write about your and your friends’ hobbies. (UNIT 1)
My favourite hobby
All of my friends play football.
Some of them like to ski.
Many play chess or piano.
They all do a lot but not me.
Yet, I have a favourite hobby.
You may think it is a curious thing.
I like to sit still in the sunshine
And listen to summer birds sing.
