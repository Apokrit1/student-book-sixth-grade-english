Unit 5: Travelling Through Time
1. Means of transport

a. Transport in Greece
Initiate a discussion about transport in Greece (means of transport, problems people face
when travelling e.t.c.) and then do the activities:
Possible answer
When you come to Greece, you can visit the islands by boat. You can also take the
aeroplane.
You can have the experience of a horse carriage on some of the islands. In the mainland,
you can travel by bus or by train. In the city of Athens, you can take also the metro or go
by taxi.
b. The Public Transport Company Survey
Invite the pupils to answer the questionnaire and finally express their opinion about the
safest means. They may justify their opinion.
