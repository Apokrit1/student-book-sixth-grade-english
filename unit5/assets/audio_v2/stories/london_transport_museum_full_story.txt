Joe wrote a letter to his friend about his school visit to the London Transport Museum in Covent Garden. He wrote: 'Last week my class went to the London Transport Museum to see the history of transportation. You can have a go at operating a tube train on the simulator. You can pull the real levers and push the buttons. In another part of the museum you can see how the tracks and the railway signal work. In the old days, horses used to pull the buses. Today you can sit in the driver's seat of a red double-decker bus. The guide showed us around and we played an exciting treasure hunt game! After the tour, we visited the museum gift shop.'
