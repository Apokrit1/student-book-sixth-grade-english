On Wednesday, November 13, 1968, Anastasia's grandmother learned interesting things during history lesson. Our teacher explained that in Ancient Greece both men and women used to wear linen in summer and warm wool in winter. Their clothes were simple tunics and warm togas. They also enjoyed using mirrors and hairbrushes. Women kept their hair long, in braids, or wore it in ponytails. Men used to have their hair short and they usually had a beard, too. Both men and women used to wear sweet perfume. How interesting!
