In the museum, the children discovered the old rules of the omnibus published in The Times on 30 January 1836. Victorian people used to travel across London on a horse-drawn omnibus. The rules stated: Keep your feet off the seats. Have your money ready when you desire to alight. Do not impose on the conductor the necessity of finding you change; he is not a banker. Do not spit on the straw. Behave respectfully to every female passenger. In modern underground tube train stations, passengers see modern safety signs: Mind the gap, keep clear of the doors, do not consume food or drink, and do not lean against the doors.
