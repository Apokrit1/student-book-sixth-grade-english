Anastasia found some pages from her grandmother's old diary from November 1968. Her grandmother wrote: 'Tuesday, November 5, 1968: I bought a nice mini skirt and a pair of high heeled shoes to wear at Angie's party on Saturday. I also bought her a present: a record of The Beatles! Sunday, November 10, 1968: Angie's party last night was fantastic! We all danced rock and roll and sang Beatles' songs. Angie was beautiful in her bell-bottomed pants and everyone seemed to admire my mini skirt. I met Andreas, her cousin. He had his hair in a ponytail. It was really cute! The food and drinks were delicious: I had a lot of canapes and fruit punch! Monday, November 11, 1968: It's too bad I can't wear my mini skirt at school, but I look nice in my blue uniform. I saw Andreas on the way from school. I was too shy to talk to him!'
