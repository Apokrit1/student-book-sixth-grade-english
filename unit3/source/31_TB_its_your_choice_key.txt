3. Class comparisons

Ask pupils to work in pairs. They may ask each other their opinion about who runs the
fastest, who sings the most beautifully etc. Use the example in their book to guide them.
Motivate them to think about other, perhaps unusual abilities that their fellow students
have and make sentences. (who jumps the highest, who reads the fastest, who dresses
the best, who remembers the best, who talks the most etc.) Finally, ask them to announce
their findings and compare with findings of other pairs.
