## Notes for the build agent

- **Shrek, Tinkerbelle, Peter Pan, Asterix, Obelix** appear in the text (see Rights in SKILL.md).
  Names in text and exercises are fine. **Never generate images, SVGs or icons depicting them.**
  Use original creatures and historical/mythological representations (Cyclops, Centaurs, Medusa, Sphinx, Puck).
- **A Midsummer Night's Dream** (SB Lesson 3) is a performance project: scaffold script, roles, and stage directions.
- **Folktale Play** "The Fifty-Cent Piece": transcribe and voice verbatim from Teacher's Book script.
