UNIT 10: Time for Fun
1. Eight Below

Instruct your pupils to read the information about the film eight below and then write an
e-mail to Kate concerning the film
KEY
Dear Kate,
I have seen “Eight Below”. It is about two Antarctic explorers who left their sled dogs
behind in order to survive in the cold. It’s an exciting adventure film. I enjoyed this
moving story about friendship, love and survival very much.
Love
XX
2. Which film?

Advise pupils to read the example and think about another well known film. They may
work in pairs or groups. They can use the clues given. The other pupils have to guess the
title of the film.
Possible film to talk about: Lion King, The Lord of the Rings, Home Alone, Harry Potter
e.t.c.
