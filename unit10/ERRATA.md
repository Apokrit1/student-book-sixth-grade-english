# Errata & Editorial Decisions — Unit 10: Time for Fun

## Document Overview
This document records editorial resolutions, print typos, layout run-ons, and pedagogical adaptations for Unit 10 (*Time for Fun*) of the 6th Grade Primary English Coursebook companion.

---

### E1: Branded Super Spy Character & Intellectual Property (James Bond / 007)
- **Source**: Pupil's Book Unit 10 Lesson 1 (pp. 107-110), "The Different Faces of a Super Spy".
- **Guideline**: References to characters, actors, and films (e.g. Sean Connery, Pierce Brosnan, Ian Fleming) are maintained as educational cultural references for studying the **Present Simple Passive** (*"A new film is directed...", "Gadgets are invented by..."*).
- **Rights Policy**: No branded character logos, proprietary film posters, or trademarked visual assets are reproduced. All visuals are 100% original bespoke vector art depicting general cinema sets, film festival activities, and youth review clubs.

### E2: Participial Adjective Contrast (-ed vs. -ing)
- **Source**: Pupil's Book Unit 10 Lesson 2 (p. 112).
- **Pedagogical Note**: Clear inductive contrast is scaffolded between:
  - `-ing` adjectives describing the characteristic/cause of a feeling (*boring, exciting, moving, terrifying*).
  - `-ed` adjectives describing the emotion experienced by a person (*bored, excited, moved, terrified*).
  - Contrasting examples prevent typical L1 Greek interference errors.

### E3: Reviewing & Critical Thinking (Film Review Workshop)
- **Source**: Pupil's Book Unit 10 Lesson 3 (p. 114) and Workbook Section B.
- **Pedagogical Treatment**:
  - Scaffolds a complete genre-based review framework: Title, Director/Actors, Genre, Setting, Plot summary (without spoiling the ending), and Final Recommendation with star ratings.
