                  showing the tremendous efforts a disabled person has to make in sports as well as in real life. With the harmony
                  of “sky, earth and human beings,” the emblem joins Chinese characters, calligraphy and the Paralympic spirit.
                  It embodies the Paralympic motto of “Spirit in Motion” and shows the integration of heart, body and spirit in
                  human beings - the core of the philosophy of Chinese culture. The three colours in the emblem represent the
