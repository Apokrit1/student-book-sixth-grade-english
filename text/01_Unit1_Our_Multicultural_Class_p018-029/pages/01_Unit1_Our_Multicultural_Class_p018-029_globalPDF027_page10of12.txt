                2. The country is mostly mountainous in Scotland and Wales but with green plains and hills in the South. Rivers

                4. The people of Britain are multicultural, coming from different countries and races, so here you can meet
                     British people from Asia, Africa or the Caribbean. They speak English and the language of their fathers as
                     well.
                5. I think it’s exciting to live in Great Britain because it opens its doors to everyone!
