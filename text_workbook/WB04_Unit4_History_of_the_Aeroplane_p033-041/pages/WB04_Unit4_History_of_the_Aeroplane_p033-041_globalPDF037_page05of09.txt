             Markos:        About 15 children were there.
             Mary:          When _____________________________________
             Markos:        The party started at 7 o’clock.
             Mary:          Were _____________________________________
             Markos:        No, Peter and Joan weren’t there. But Julie was there, and I danced with her.
             Mary:          What _________________________________ like?
             Markos:        The music was fantastic. Very danceable.
             Mary:          Did _______________________________________
             Markos:        No, I didn’t eat much.
